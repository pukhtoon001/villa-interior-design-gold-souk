Planning Tips Before You Start

Start with the rooms you use every day. Family living areas and the kitchen usually deliver the biggest improvement in daily comfort, and they help set the colour and material direction for the rest of the villa. Decide early how much storage you need, since joinery has the longest lead time of any part of the project.

Think about Dubai's climate when you choose materials. Surfaces that handle heat, sunlight and humidity without fading or warping will look better for longer and cost less to maintain. If you are renovating an older villa, it is also worth reviewing wiring, plumbing and air-conditioning layouts before the new finishes go in, so you upgrade the hidden parts once instead of twice.

Finally, work with a team that can handle both design and execution. When the same company plans and builds your interior, decisions move faster, drawings match what gets built, and there is one point of responsibility if something needs to be adjusted.
